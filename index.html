About

I am an emerging multidisciplinary professional moving fluidly between commercial polish and mission-driven storytelling, and I move between disciplines on purpose. To me, design is less about mastering a single skill and more so a way of asking questions. Sometimes the answer is a system with rules and consistency. Sometimes it's a graphic that doesn't need any words at all.

I've never believed a career has to read like a straight line;, and most people don't think that way. My portfolio zigzags between commercial work and projects that matter to me for reasons that have nothing to do with a resume. No matter what, I start from the same place: who is this for, what do they need to understand, and what is the clearest way to get them there?
